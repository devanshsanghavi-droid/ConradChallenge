## 2026-10-07, iteration 4, task 14: what the model accounts for, in the app, the PDF, the journal and the README (simulation only)

- **The answer to the manager's four points, in one place** (`residualmap/capabilities.py`, new). The app's 'What this model accounts for' table has one row each for water age, organics and chlorine demand, chlorine type and temperature: what the model does, how it was tested in simulation, and what it does not do or what was tested and not adopted. Every result number in it is read from `outputs/` when the app runs (`SOURCES`, a file and key per number; settings and bars are fixed text), the adoption words follow the outputs' own adoption flags, and the temperature and organics rows name the assumed schedules and truth constants behind their numbers. Water age and the chloramine mode are adopted (tasks 9 and 12); temperature (tasks 10 and 10b) and organics (task 11) were tested and not adopted, so both are 'absorbed into the calibrated decay rates' refitted from the last three months of samples; the two-reactant audit (task 13) is a measurement. The wording rule: 'accounts for' only where there is a term and a test; 'not modelled' for nitrification as a process, blending and pH.
- **The PDF** gains four chemistry lines: the disinfectant and the measured species (free or total chlorine; in chloramine mode also the prior's pH and Cl2:N and the nitrification-watch count), the minimum residual and its source (0.2 mg/L free chlorine 'a common operating minimum' or 0.5 mg/L total chlorine 'a common utility operating target', each 'not a California rule (California requires a detectable residual)', or 'set by you'), how the seasons are handled (water temperature and TOC are not inputs; absorbed into the decay rates refitted every three months), and 'Validated in simulation only'. No kb20 or temperature field: no temperature model was adopted.
- **Also in the app:** help text on the threshold, with Texas as an example (30 TAC 290.110); a grid-edge warning when more than half of the calibration sits on the lowest or highest bulk or wall decay rate of the grid (`capabilities.grid_edge_warning`; for free chlorine at the 0.10 per day floor it points to tasks 10b, 11 and 13); a labelled fallback for the chloramine demo truth when EPANET-MSX cannot run (task 12's first-order twin, with a warning that it flatters the model; the failure is cached, so MSX is tried once per network, dose and scenario); and the chloramine grid's cache tag in `prepare()`'s cache key.
