# Changelog

## 2026-09-21 — iteration 3, task 4: route optimiser

- `residualmap/route.py`: `plan_route(model, K)` picks K (junction, hour) pairs at once, greedily on the daily-minimum straddle score (1.96·sd − |μ − ln 0.2| of ln(daily min)), with a hydraulic-distance repulsion `repulsion · exp(−d_nearest_chosen / ℓ)`; the hour is the daytime hour with the largest reducible sd at that junction, at most ⌈K/11⌉ sites per hour so one person can drive it. Each site carries a one-sentence reason (used by the app in task 6). Baselines `random_route` (K random junctions) and `demand_route` (K highest base-demand junctions — "sample where the customers are"), both on an evenly spread 08:00–16:00 schedule.
- The route is planned from the `.inp` alone: `SimGP24.fit_prior()` (uniform weights over the 675 × 5 grid, no GP). "Upload your model, get your first route."
- Repulsion set by a spatial statistic, not by recall: repulsion 1.0 with ℓ = 5% of the network's hydraulic diameter gives the optimised route the same mean nearest-neighbour spacing as a random route on Net3 (2.2 / 1.8 / 1.3 km at K = 5 / 8 / 12; random 2.1 / 1.9 / 1.3). With the first setting (0.5) the route clustered in the borderline zone (0.2–0.3 km apart), which hurt the calibration (K=12 coverage 0.81, RMSE 0.10).
- `experiment.run_routes` + `outputs/results_routes_Net3.csv`, `outputs/route_comparison_Net3.png`, `summary_Net3.json["routes_daily_min"]`, `["route_scenario0_K8"]` (the 8 sites with their reasons). The figure shows missed night violations summed over the 8 scenario-months rather than recall, because recall sits at 0.97–1.0 for every route and is not legible.

New row `simgp_core_nogp` = the calibrated simulator alone, to see what the discrepancy GP absorbs. Figure `outputs/stress_test_Net3.png`.
- **The initial-level perturbation is a no-op on the scored day.** The truth is a 7-day run scored on day 7; a tank's initial level is forgotten by then: max |Δ truth| on Net3 is 0.003–0.05 mg/L, no junction changes category in any seed. Only the closed pipe bites, and the coin gave it to 2 of 8 seeds (pipes 323 and 199: up to 0.27 and 0.64 mg/L change, 16 and 62 junctions moved by > 0.05 mg/L). Reported as such; the `"persistent"` variant is what the stress test needs: 0.46–0.82 mg/L peak change and 26–77 junctions moved in every seed.

## 2026-09-21 — iteration 3, task 2: close the calibration gap

- Grid: two hydraulic-mismatch axes, demand ×{0.85, 1.0, 1.15} and Hazen-Williams C ×{0.9, 1.0, 1.1} (`simulate_nominal_chlorine(demand_mult, rough_mult)`, `simgp.GRIDS["full"]`): 675 EPANET runs, 19 s on Net3, cached at `outputs/cache/grid24_full_Net3.pkl` (6 MB, float32). The iteration-2 grid stays as `grid="decay"`.
- Source-dose axis ×{0.90, 0.95, 1.00, 1.05, 1.10}: exact and free (first-order decay is linear in concentration, so it is a ln-offset; `grid_dose_weights` puts the joint posterior over (member, dose)). Found because in seed 2 the junctions next to the source sat at 1.28 mg/L all day (truth dose +7%) while every member said 1.19 with a 0.1 mg/L band.
- Local hydraulic error (`local_hydraulic_var`): calibrating the global multipliers does not remove the operator's per-node demand (±15%) and per-pipe roughness (±10%) errors, which are the size of the axes; the grid's own spread across the hydraulic axes at each (hour, junction), averaged over the decay posterior, is added to the predictive variance. In the daily-minimum Monte Carlo it enters as the whole-day deviation of a random hydraulic sibling of the drawn member, so the minimum is taken over a coherent day.
- Likelihood scale `LIK_SD` 0.25 → 0.35 in ln C: the day-time RMS mismatch between the truth and the best grid member on Net3 is 0.33, i.e. the scale is the model error, not the grab-sample noise. Scan 0.30/0.35/0.40/0.50: coverage rises smoothly (0.88/0.90/0.91/0.93), RMSE and recall flat — no lucky spot. Marginalising the scale over a list was tried and rejected: the tightest scale wins whenever a few samples fit one member, which concentrates the posterior further (coverage 0.81–0.84).
- Student-t likelihood is now the default for `SimGP` too (the task-1 diagnosis applies to any rule that samples the hydraulic fronts; with Gaussian the straddle rule lost recall at n=10–12).
- `SimGP24` discrepancy GP: the inputs that vary with the hour (age at that hour, sin, cos) get a length-scale floor of 1.0 standardised ≈ 3 h. Before, they often sat at the 0.1 bound (≈ 15 min), the joint 24-h draws were white noise and the Monte-Carlo minimum of 24 noisy values was biased low by ~2 sd: 21% of unsampled junctions sat ABOVE the band, half of those with a true daily minimum > 0.5 mg/L. Age-scaled discrepancy amplitude was tried and rejected (mean nominal age on Net3 is 15.1 h at 02:00 vs 16.4 h at 14:00 — the night mismatch is tank/demand timing, not age).
- Acquisition uses the reducible predictive sd (`z_sd_acq`: parameter spread + GP, without the local hydraulic term that no sample can shrink). With the full sd the max-uncertainty rule chased the hydraulic fronts and collapsed to recall 0.52 at n=15.
- `outputs/reliability_Net3.png`: nominal vs empirical coverage at 50/80/90/95%, iteration 2 vs now, snapshot and daily minimum. New metric columns `coverage50/80/95`, `recall_all` (violations found by the sample itself or by a flag).

## 2026-09-21 — iteration 3, task 1: time-aware model

- `simgp.simulator_grid_24h`: the simulator grid now keeps all 24 hours of every EPANET run (cache `outputs/cache/grid24_<net>.pkl`; `simulator_grid` slices its hour from it, so iteration-2 numbers are unchanged).
- `simgp.SimGP24`: samples are (junction, hour, mg/L). Grid members are scored on the simulated value at each sample's own hour; the discrepancy GP takes age-at-that-hour, the hydraulic embedding, wall index, distance to source and sin/cos(hour). The daily minimum and P(daily min < 0.2) are Monte-Carlo: draw a grid member by weight (whole-day profile) + a joint 24-h GP draw per junction, take the minimum (256 draws).
- `simgp.grid_weights`: calibration likelihood is now Student-t (ν=3, scale 0.25) for the time-aware model. Diagnosis on Net3 seed 0: the straddle rule samples junctions near 0.2 mg/L, which are the fronts where the operator's model is structurally wrong (junctions 20, 131, 151: truth 3× every grid member); under a Gaussian four such readings pulled the calibration to kb=0.1 and daily-min recall fell from 0.88 (n=4) to 0.62 (n=15). Under Student-t, on the straddle loop over 4 seeds at n=15: RMSE 0.11 vs 0.19, precision 0.85 vs 0.64, coverage 0.75 vs 0.29. `SimGP` keeps `lik="gauss"` by default so iteration-2 results stand; the option is shared.
- `surrogate.acquire_time`: (junction, hour) acquisition restricted to 07:00–17:00: random, max-uncertainty, hourly straddle, and `straddle_min` (straddle on the daily minimum picks the junction; the daytime hour with the largest predictive sd picks the hour).
- `experiment.run_scenario_time` + figures `outputs/curves_time_Net3.png`, `outputs/day_vs_night_predicted_Net3.png`; numbers in `outputs/results_time_Net3.csv` and `summary_Net3.json["time_aware_daily_min"]`. Baselines: mean of samples; the iteration-2 model treating every sample as a 14:00 sample ("time-blind"), scored against the daily minimum.
- Net3, 8 seeds, daytime samples only, scored on unsampled junctions, target = daily minimum: `straddle_min` recall 0.86 (n=3) → 0.94 (n=8) → 0.93 (n=15), precision 0.94 at n=15, F1 0.93; RMSE of the daily minimum 0.08 → 0.12 → 0.13 mg/L; recall at the 22:00 snapshot 0.78 → 0.95 → 0.85. Per-seed recall at n=15 (`straddle_min`): 1.0, 0.75, 0.88, 0.82, 1.0, 1.0, 1.0, 1.0. **Acceptance (recall ≥ 0.80 at 15 daytime samples): met.**
- Calibration recovers the physics from daytime samples: posterior mode at n=15 averages kb 0.31 /day, kw 0.75 m/day, γ 0.94 (truth 0.40, 0.70, 1.0).
- Iteration-2 snapshot numbers re-verified in this environment: identical to 4 decimals (Python 3.13, WNTR 1.5.0, scikit-learn 1.9.1). PINN and rich-feature GP rows drift (L-BFGS / 23-dim kernel optimiser), nothing else.

The demand/roughness axes of task 2 are the intended fix.
